## Material findings

1. **Process-graph launch/request enumeration omits mandatory AF/Workflow Validation-loop validators and the AF-scope A-loop advisor — contradicts locked Q21/Q22 boundaries and breaks deny-generation executability.**
   - Locked behavior: Validation-loop is **mandatory** at every AF completion, every Workflow composition join/final, and Process final completion, owned by a Planning/Validation-tier validator (plan line 53, line 221; Q21=B/Q22=B). A-loop two-clean is mandatory at every AF completion, executed by the leaf Step immediately before AF V (line 220; Q14).
   - But the control-plane graph that §6 uses to generate deny-all rules enumerates: "Process launches the top Workflow **and final validator**. Workflow requests nested Workflow, owned AF, **composition verifier**, advisor, or defect escalation. **AF requests Steps, AF verifier, or defect escalation**" (line 163; mirrored in §1 line 127: Workflow may "request a verifier/advisor"; AF "requests Steps and AF verifiers"). Line 164's deny-all leaf controller classes are "Final, triage, advisor, defect-escalation, and Iterate controllers" — no scope-validator class at all.
   - Consequences as written: (a) no request/launch edge exists for an **AF-scope validator** or a **Workflow-scope (composition-join) validator** — only the Process final validator is launched; (b) no edge exists for the **AF/leaf-Step A-loop advisor** — the Workflow's advisor edge serves the Workflow's own scope, and the executor's free Advisor-consult right is explicitly scoped to `i_running` (line 87), while A-loop runs after `i_two_clean`. An implementer generating "explicit deny-all leaves from the hierarchy" (line 262) and rejecting "non-Process / unreachable / missing-bound" nodes (line 137) from this enumeration would fence out control-plane children that locked decisions make mandatory — a deadlock between §1/§3 and §5/Locked, not a wording nit.
   - Fix direction (not applied this turn): extend the §1/§3 enumerations with per-scope validator + advisor request edges, **or** state explicitly and symmetrically that all scope A-loop advisors and Validation-loop validators are Authorizer-launched control-plane children (matching the "equivalent Authorizer-launched Mentors/Validators" phrasing at line 86) with the launch trigger bound to the ordinary SM edges (`a_two_clean →` AF validator admission, `v_verified →` scope validator admission).
