# Research Scope: Canonical SDLC Process Architecture

## Research Question
What is the canonical industry-standard software development process architecture as practiced by leading software organizations as of mid-2026?

## Mode
Ultradeep — AF-DECIDE / FS-SILVER_DEEP_RESEARCH

## In Scope
- SDLC practices from Google, Microsoft, Amazon/AWS, Meta, Apple, Netflix, Spotify, Stripe, GitHub, Atlassian, Shopify, Cloudflare, LinkedIn, Uber, Airbnb
- DORA metrics and capabilities (Accelerate State of DevOps)
- NIST SSDF 800-218 Secure Software Development Framework
- OWASP Top 10 and application security practices
- CNCF/OpenSSF supply chain security standards
- Platform engineering and Internal Developer Platforms (IDPs)
- AI-assisted/agentic software engineering (with credible operational adoption evidence)
- Observability (OpenTelemetry) and SRE practices
- DevEx measurement (SPACE framework, DX surveys)
- All 18 required Process Areas
- Workflow-level detail with metrics, RACI, anti-patterns
- Implementation tiers (minimum / standard / leading-edge)

## Out of Scope
- Hardware development lifecycle
- Non-software product development
- Industry-specific compliance domains not covered by NIST/OWASP/CNCF
- Fully autonomous AI development without human oversight (no credible evidence)
- Company-specific proprietary processes not publicly documented

## Assumptions
- Mid-2026 represents the current state of practice for this research date (2026-07-12)
- "Leading organizations" refers to publicly documented practices from named companies
- Practices tagged as "universal" have ≥2 independent credible sources
- "Emerging" practices have ≤1 year of operational evidence at scale
