## Triangulation Approach

1. **Universal practices** — Found in 5+ organizations independently
2. **Leading-edge** — Documented by 2-3 organizations, emerging pattern
3. **Context-dependent** — Varies by company size, industry, regulation
4. **Emerging** — Single-source or early adoption, needs more evidence

## Quality Gates

- Minimum 30 sources with credibility >75/100
- 3+ independent sources per major claim
- All 18 process areas covered with evidence
- Each workflow has all required fields
- No invented areas without evidence
- Enterprise handbook quality

## Risk Mitigation

1. **Insufficient sources for AI practices** — Tag as emerging, note evidence gaps
2. **Proprietary practices not documented** — Focus on publicly shared practices
3. **Recency of information** — Prioritize 2024-2026 sources, note older sources
4. **Bias toward large companies** — Include startup context variations explicitly
5. **Tool-specific vs practice-specific** — Focus on practices, tool categories only
