## Phase 2: Evidence Triangulation

All major claims triangulated across >=2 independent sources:
- DORA research + company blog posts for practice validation
- Multiple company blogs for cross-company pattern confirmation
- Survey data (GitHub, Stack Overflow, DORA) for adoption statistics
- Standards bodies (NIST, OWASP, OpenSSF) for security practice validation

## Phase 3: Synthesis and Writing

- Process area taxonomy derived from evidence, not invented
- Workflows mapped to observed practices at leading organizations
- Practice classification based on adoption breadth and maturity
- Maturity model grounded in DORA capability levels
