# Critique Report

## Strengths of This Research

1. **Strong evidence base**: DORA research (10+ years, academically rigorous) provides the backbone
2. **Cross-source triangulation**: All major claims supported by >=2 independent sources
3. **Industry breadth**: Covers Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, Netflix, Spotify, Stripe, GitHub, Atlassian, Shopify, Cloudflare, LinkedIn, Airbnb
4. **Standards alignment**: Grounded in NIST SSDF, OWASP, OpenSSF, CNCF
5. **AI integration**: Reflects 2025-2026 reality with 90%+ AI adoption
6. **Maturity tiers**: Implementation guidance at minimum/standard/leading-edge levels

## Weaknesses and Limitations

1. **Self-reporting bias**: Survey data (DORA, GitHub, Stack Overflow) relies on self-reported perceptions
2. **Survivorship bias**: Company blogs document successes, not failures
3. **Vendor influence**: Some sources (Humanitec, Atlassian) have vendor bias in their content
4. **Rapidly evolving AI landscape**: AI practices may shift significantly within 6-12 months
5. **Context dependency**: What works at Google/Netflix may not transfer to smaller organizations
6. **Limited regulated-industry evidence**: Most sources are from consumer tech, not healthcare/finance/government
7. **Conflation of DevOps and SRE**: Some sources treat these as interchangeable, others as distinct

## Gaps in Evidence

1. **Quantitative ROI for specific practices**: DORA metrics predict performance, but specific ROI calculations for individual practices are limited
2. **Small-team practices**: Most evidence is from large-scale organizations; adaptation guidance for <10-person teams is thin
3. **Non-cloud-native contexts**: Limited evidence for on-premises, mainframe, or embedded systems
4. **Cross-functional team dynamics**: RACI is well-defined for technical workflows but less clear for product/design collaboration
5. **Post-AI quality metrics**: No consensus yet on how to measure code quality when AI generates significant portions

## Recommendations for Improvement

1. Include more regulated-industry evidence (healthcare, finance, government)
2. Develop explicit adaptation guidance for different organizational sizes
3. Track AI practice evolution quarterly given rapid pace of change
4. Add quantitative case studies with specific ROI numbers
5. Include anti-pattern documentation based on failure case studies
