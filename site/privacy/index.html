Privacy Policy

How Alo Labs handles information when you use sb.alolabs.dev and Silver Bullet.

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Silver Bullet is designed as a local-first plugin: enforcement runs in your coding-agent session on your machine. We do not operate a Silver Bullet cloud backend that receives your source code, prompts, or session transcripts.

1. No telemetry from Silver Bullet

The Silver Bullet plugin does not include analytics or telemetry that reports your usage to Alo Labs. The product positioning “No Telemetry” reflects this design. Optional recommended tools (for example Graphify or agentmemory) are configured by you in your own environment and are outside this policy unless you enable them.

2. Website and external assets (sb.alolabs.dev)

This static site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process standard server logs (such as IP address, user agent, and requested URL) for security and operations. Pages also request IBM Plex Mono from Google Fonts and Lucide’s icon script from the unpkg CDN. Those requests disclose ordinary request metadata, including your IP address, user agent, referrer, and requested asset, to the respective providers under their own privacy policies. We do not use the Silver Bullet site to build advertising profiles or sell personal data.

3. Local storage in your browser

The site may store your theme preference (light or dark) in localStorage on your device. Help Center search may index public documentation in your browser for offline-style lookup. This data stays on your device unless you clear it.

4. Third-party coding agents

When you use Silver Bullet inside Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, or another host, that host’s privacy policy governs how your prompts and code are processed. Silver Bullet adds hooks and skills locally; it does not replace or override your host’s data practices.

5. Source availability and GitHub

Source code and issues are published on GitHub under BUSL 1.1. If you contribute or open issues, GitHub’s terms and privacy policy apply to information you submit there.

6. Children

The site and software are intended for developers and engineering teams. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as the product or hosting changes. The “Last updated” date will reflect revisions.

8. Contact

Privacy questions may be directed to Alo Labs via alolabs.dev or the project repository.
