Overview

/silver:deep-research-multi-ai is the explicit multi-model variant of canonical deep research. The host drives the research phases once; eligible retrieval, triangulation, and critique work is delegated through AF-MULTI-AI-TASK.

Use canonical /silver:deep-research by default. Select this workflow when parallel independent evidence gathering justifies the added cost and reconciliation work.

Catalog Composition

CLARIFY → DECIDE → MULTI_AI_TASK → DOCUMENT → VALIDATE

The composition preserves the normal research contracts. Multi-AI dispatch does not bypass need-profile capture, source quality rules, deterministic consolidation, or final validation.

Research Modes

  • General research: parallel retrieval, triangulation, and critique contributions.
  • Solution landscape: evidence-backed candidate inventory and weighted comparison artifacts.
  • Solution compare: two or more named solutions evaluated against the accepted need profile.

Artifacts

The canonical run_manifest.json uses manifest v4 and spine: multi-ai-task-v2. Consolidation can emit report.html, landscape-report.html, and comparison JSON, Markdown, and spreadsheet artifacts. Older v3 manifests migrate copy-on-write with an audit chain.
